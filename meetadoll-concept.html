Good to know

Vendor FAQ

How do I reserve a stall?
Tap any Reserve button to chat with us on WhatsApp, or create an account and pick your tier. Your stall is held for 15 minutes while you complete payment securely via Paystack.
What's the difference between Tier 1 and Tier 2?
Tier 1 (₦250,000) is for fashion, beauty and lifestyle vendors in prime hall positions. Tier 2 (₦210,000) is for food and drinks vendors in the dedicated food court zone. Both include table, chairs, setup space and 2 vendor passes for both days.
Can I get a refund if I cancel?
Refund terms are stated in our Terms & Conditions — please read them before paying. Chat with us on WhatsApp and we'll walk you through the policy for your situation.
When can vendors set up?
Setup details, load-in times and stall rules are shared with confirmed vendors ahead of the event. Gates open to shoppers at 10:00 AM daily.
Is there an entry fee for shoppers?
Follow @meetadoll_exhibition on Instagram and TikTok for ticketing and gate announcements as the date approaches.
